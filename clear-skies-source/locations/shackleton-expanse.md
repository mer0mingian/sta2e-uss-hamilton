**The Shackleton Expanse** is a large region of unexplored space in the Beta Quadrant.  Only one millionth of the expanse has been explored.

## Description
It is nominally claimed by the United Federation of Planets.  However, no other entities recognize their claim, and the Federation does not press it.  It is located on the opposite side of Klingon space from the rest of the Federation. It is connected by a narrow corridor, but the most direct route is to fly through Klingon space. It was granted to the Federation by the Klingon Empire. It borders both the Klingon Empire and the Romulan Star Empire. The only permanent outpost is Narendra Station, a joint effort of the Klingon Empire and the United Federation of Planets.

The [Shackleton Expanse](shackleton-expanse.md) is known for having frequent gravimetric and spatial anomalies.  They appear and disappear frequently, seemingly at random.  They pose a navigation hazard to any ship flying through the expanse, as collision with an anomaly will damage a ship's shields.  Contact with an anomaly while unshielded can destroy a hull.

Federation ships operating in the expanse coat their hulls in [Trellium D](https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Trellium), a mineral which protects ship's hulls from the anomalies.  The only known source of Trellium D is located in the [memoryalpha:Delphic Expanse](https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Delphic_Expanse), another region of space formerly known for spatial anomalies.  As the Delphic Expanse is located deep within Federation territory, obtaining Trellium D is difficult for ships from other entities (e.g., the Romulan Star Empire).
