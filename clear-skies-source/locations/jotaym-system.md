The **Jotaym System** is an area of space located in the Shackleton Expanse. It contains a particle fountain. In 2381, [USS Ross](../ships/uss-ross.md) entered into first contact with the [J'Shashia](../characters/jshashia.md) on [Space Ring Observer](space-ring-observer.md), a station built to observe the fountain.
