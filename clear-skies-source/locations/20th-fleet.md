The **20th Fleet** is a small United Federation of Planets fleet.  Its sphere of operations is the [Shackleton Expanse](shackleton-expanse.md).  It is lead by Vice Admiral April Hebert, who keeps her flag aboard Narendra Station.  Its primary mission is peaceful exploration of the Shackleton Expanse.
