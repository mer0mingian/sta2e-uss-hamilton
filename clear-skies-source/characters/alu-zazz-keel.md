**Alu Zazz Keel** is the High Regent of [Jateran](../locations/jateran.md), played by Eric Campbell.

## Description
Keel is the political leader of the [J'Shashia](jshashia.md). He once described his office as "largely ceremonial". He is very jovial and welcoming to strangers.
