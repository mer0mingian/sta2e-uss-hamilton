## Core adaptation changes
1. **Single-evening length:** Scenario must be finishable in ~3.5–4 hours play time. Drastically shorten plot/content.
2. **Skip planetary politics:** Remove the whole plot around political affairs on the planet. Crew learns of the crystalline entity shortly after arrival. Core focus: help it safely out of the planet and find it a suitable nursery.
3. **Main antagonists:** Tholians — the same ones from scenario **"A Starless Night"** in campaign continuity: Tholian dreadnaught **T’Laru**, Captain **Vozrax** (Föderationsstandard: „Assembler“). Motives remain ambiguous (they may wish to support “nursing” the entity, but may also act aggressively).
4. **Nursery planet entity:** Nursery ist ein **Planet mit hohem Pflanzenanteil, aber keinem tierischen Leben** (geeignet, aber bewusst rätselhaft). Tholians können Interesse an „Nursing“/Schutz haben und den Ort als Zuflucht/Aufzucht kennen; Herkunft, genaue Eignung und tholianische Überlieferung bleiben mysteriös. Die Tholians sind nicht die Entität.
5. **Player characters (from Player CSV):** Use USS Hamilton players from `sta2e-uss-hamilton/Player Character Data.csv`. Available players at table: **Captain, CMO, Operations Officer, Chief Engineer**. **Security Chief** is taking a break (not present). **First Officer** is available to be played by the GM.
6. **Supporting NPC life:** Include comments/discussions from NPCs supporting the crew to make scenes more alive. New characters may be introduced. Must include the **Cetacean Ops officer: Lt. Maren** (Delphin, Cetacean Ops Navigation/Science-Support, formelle Wortwahl, melodische Stimme). Note which NPCs already exist in the Hamilton repo (`sta2e-uss-hamilton/`) when selecting/reusing NPCs.
7. **Language:** Provided content must be in German, except game terms (for example stats, talents, species abilities) which stay in their game-term form. Focuses may be in German. Names (ships, characters, places) may stay in English; GM may translate on the fly.

## Mission frame and entry (revised 2026-11-17)
- **Continuity basis:** Supportive Source + Campaign Principles + Hamilton materials/logs (archived/played “Eine sternenlose Nacht” not automatic continuity).
- **Setting premise:** Jateran (Lithium mining colony outside Federation space, known species, prior Federation contact, split hemispheres with one barren from an earlier entity). Crystalline entity maturation produces Dilithium. **Earthquakes increase** and correlate with subspace pulses.
- **Hook/order:** Vozrax transmits to Starfleet Command only **T’Laru**, **Captain Malax**, **stardate**, and **location (Jateran)**. Admiral Blackwater issues the resulting order to USS Hamilton.
- **Entry:** Play begins with a **computer log entry by Commander Knox** (First Officer) recording the message and Blackwater’s order — fast start with little crew knowledge. Then **Babyparty** (character play; Klingon/Vulcan details to be aligned with players). Early teaser: intensity of Klingon contractions/labor. **Gi’ral** seeks water/calm during pregnancy and meets **Lt. Maren** in the Cetacean-Ops/tank area (quiet character moment; Maren: formal wording, melodious voice).
- **Pulses/urgency:** On arrival Hamilton detects a first subspace pulse like a **contraction (“Wehe”)**, not interpretable. **Multiple pulses follow at shortening intervals**, indicating when the entity will break out of the planet’s surface. Stardate adds urgency/curiosity through uncertainty.
- **Goal/challenge:** Enable the entity’s emergence **without destroying the planet**, then bring it to the defined **Nursery** (planet with abundant plant life, no animal life; mysterious, possible Tholian nursing/protection interest). Crew forms hypotheses after arrival (earthquakes increasing, matching pulses). **Pulse/resonance recognition:** primary for **T'Muk** or **Alpha-Crew**; **Lt. Maren** is backup and may provide the documented hint if needed.
- **Tholian motive:** Vozrax trusts Malax (prior cooperation), seeks to keep peace and present as protector of the godlike-revered entity. Tholians view the Enterprise-D as having “murdered” a crystalline entity (Tholian perspective).
