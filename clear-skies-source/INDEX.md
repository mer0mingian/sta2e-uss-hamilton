# Clear Skies — Abridged Reference

Local reference pack for a private **Star Trek Adventures 2nd Edition** pen-and-paper adaptation of the abridged *Clear Skies* plot, played with the **USS Hamilton** crew instead of the USS Ross crew.

Source material is the fan-run [Clear Skies RPG Wiki](https://clear-skies-rpg.fandom.com/wiki/Main_Page) (Fandom). The original campaign follows the USS *Ross*; this pack keeps the original framing intact and does **not** assert any canon changes. Swap the crew/ship identity when running it for USS *Hamilton*.

## Coverage & gaps

### Followed-link rationale

- Seed pages: **J'Shashia**, **Jateran**, **USS Ross** (task-specified).
- Episode run: the seed pages point at the J'Shashia arc (09–14) and the Jateran aftermath (34–35); scope was extended to the full forward arc **Episode 11 onward through Episode 59** as requested.
- Key arc NPCs: **Keah Nobal** (coup leader), **Alu Zazz Keel** (High Regent), **Yugoss** (J'Shashian scientist).
- Places directly tied to the arc: **Shackleton Expanse**, **Jotaym System**, **Space Ring Observer**.
- Ship directly tied to the arc: **Ketikess** (Tholian dreadnought).
- **20th Fleet** fetched for the Ross assignment context.

### Intentionally skipped / not fetched

- **USS Ross senior staff and auxiliary crew** (Azeri Sull, EXEO, M'Qrell, Jane Lakat, Theq Lynadas, Yeri Prahl, Zhyliss Vrenn, Olyn Madzhin-Nil, Debluch Marrone, Sup Crew, etc.) — out of scope: the adaptation uses the USS Hamilton crew.
- **Episodes 01–08 detail pages** — summaries only via the episode index.
- **Blood of the Void** episodes, **Graduation Day** specials, **The Search for Skrag** — separate series/specials.
- **Nios sector** — the wiki reports the page does not exist.
- Romulan/Klingon/Gorn/Dominion/etc. faction pages and other alien species — not linked from the seed arc pages.
- External Memory Alpha / Wikipedia pages — left as outbound links only.
- Wiki categories, templates, talk/history pages.

### Known gaps / caveats

- Episodes **48, 49, 51–59** are stubs or very short on the source wiki; their files reflect that (mostly infobox plus trivia).
- Pages with no meaningful art on the wiki (several stubs used the default box logo) have no image downloaded.
- The wiki text is the primary actual-play record, not a published script; some spellings/names are inconsistent there and were preserved.
- Three loose Tholian `.webp` files (`Tholian Captain.webp`, `Tholian Captain 2.webp`, `tholian dreadnought.webp`) sit at this folder's root. They were **not** created by this collection and were left untouched.
