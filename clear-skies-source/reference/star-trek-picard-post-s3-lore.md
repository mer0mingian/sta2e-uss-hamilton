# Star Trek: Picard — Post–Season 3 Era Lore (GM Reference)

- **Purpose:** General setting reference for a private Star Trek Adventures 2e game set after *Star Trek: Picard* Season 3.
- **Scope:** Early 25th century, focused on events and status established in 2401 (Season 3) and the immediate aftermath (~2402, one year later). Concise, GM-usable; avoids deep episode-by-episode spoilers beyond setting-defining facts.
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-16

## Quick overview (at-a-glance)

- **Timeframe anchor:** Frontier Day 2401 — 250th anniversary of *Enterprise* NX-01's launch. Crisis occurs in 2401; epilogue jumps ~1 year later.
- **Federation/Starfleet:** Survives a near-catastrophic infiltration and fleet takeover; leadership, medical, and security reforms follow (transporter purge of Borg DNA, detection of remaining Changeling infiltrators).
- **Borg (original Collective):** The remnants of the original Borg Collective, led by a dying Borg Queen, are destroyed over Jupiter when her cube is destroyed. The long Borg threat to the Federation effectively ends.
- **Changelings:** A rogue Changeling faction (separate from the Great Link's post–Dominion War peace) allies with the Borg and infiltrates Starfleet at high levels; the conspiracy is exposed, but survivors/infiltrators may remain a security concern.
- **Key ships/people (aftermath):** USS *Titan*-A is rechristened **USS *Enterprise*-G** (NCC-1701-G) with Seven of Nine as captain, Raffi Musiker as first officer, Jack Crusher as special counselor (Starfleet ensign). USS *Enterprise*-D is returned to the Fleet Museum.
- **Tone:** Recovery after existential crisis; renewed trust must be rebuilt inside Starfleet and with allies; exploration and legacy continue, but vigilance against hidden threats remains.

## Setting state (post–Season 3)

### United Federation of Planets and Starfleet
- **Frontier Day 2401:** Starfleet unveils fleetwide **Fleet Formation** (networked ship control/synchronization) during celebrations at Earth. This capability is subverted during the crisis.
- **Institutional damage:** Starfleet command and fleet units are compromised by infiltration and assimilation during the Battle of Frontier Day; many personnel are killed or traumatized. Recovery requires command continuity, personnel screening, and fleet repair/replacement.
- **Post-crisis response:** Starfleet Medical (under Admiral Beverly Crusher, per epilogue) modifies transporters to remove implanted Borg technology/DNA markers from affected personnel and develops means to expose remaining Changeling infiltrators. Full details of political fallout are not exhaustively shown on screen; GMs may treat internal security reviews and rebuilding as ongoing.
- **Operational posture:** Starfleet remains the Federation's exploratory and defensive service. The rechristening/launch of *Enterprise*-G signals continuity and a forward-looking mission despite recent trauma.

### The Borg
- **Status:** The original Borg Collective's surviving Queen and her last cube are destroyed in 2401 over Jupiter. This is presented as the final end of that 35+ year menace to the Federation.
- **Mechanism of the 2401 crisis:** Latent Borg biological technology traced to Picard's assimilation as Locutus (and inherited/passed to Jack Crusher) is exploited; Changeling-assisted infiltration enables its distribution through transporter systems to younger Starfleet officers, who are remotely assimilated during Frontier Day.
- **GM note:** "Borg are gone forever" is a strong in-universe conclusion in Picard S3, but Star Trek often revisits threats in new forms. For a post-S3 game, treat original-Collective Borg as defeated while allowing uncertainty, remnants, or unrelated adaptations only if deliberately introduced.

### Changelings (Dominion legacy)
- **Rogue faction:** A breakaway Changeling group (led in S3 by Vadic) rejects the post–Dominion War peace and seeks revenge on the Federation for abuses (including Project Proteus experimentation). They cooperate with the Borg Queen for mutual vengeance.
- **Infiltration:** Advanced infiltration reaches high levels of Starfleet and related infrastructure; some operatives are captured or killed during/after the crisis, but "remaining infiltrators" are explicitly a concern addressed by new detection methods.
- **Great Link / Dominion:** The broader Dominion/Great Link is not the active antagonist of S3's crisis; the threat is the rogue faction. Relations with the Dominion remain background context rather than a reopened war in this specific arc.

### Sol system and Earth
- **Battle of Frontier Day (2401):** Assimilated Starfleet ships threaten Earth after Sol defenses (including Sol Station/planetary defenses in the engagement) are overwhelmed; Earth faces orbital bombardment until the Borg command signal is severed.
- **Aftermath:** Earth and the Federation capital endure a severe crisis but are saved. Rebuilding, memorialization, and security hardening are reasonable background conditions for stories set soon after.

### Technology and institutions (usable hooks)
- **Fleet Formation/networked fleet control:** Demonstrated and catastrophically abused in 2401; likely subject to review, safeguards, or restricted use afterward (GM discretion unless later canon specifies).
- **Transporters:** Central attack vector in 2401; post-crisis medical/technical countermeasures remove Borg elements and help identify infiltrators.
- **USS *Enterprise*-G:** Successor in the *Enterprise* lineage (renamed *Titan*-A), crewed by a new generation of officers under Seven of Nine — a strong symbol for post-crisis exploration stories.

## Themes and table tone
- **After survival:** Relief mixed with grief, suspicion, and duty to rebuild.
- **Legacy and succession:** TNG-era figures pass the torch; younger officers step forward.
- **Trust and verification:** Infiltration trauma makes identity checks and loyalty questions plausible story elements.
- **Hope with caution:** The Federation endures and explores onward, but recent events justify preparedness.

## Uncertainties / GM discretion
- Exact political, economic, and fleet-wide reconstruction details after 2401 are only lightly depicted; define local consequences to fit your campaign.
- Status of other quadrants/powers (Klingon Empire, Romulan successor state, Cardassians, Ferengi, etc.) in the immediate post-2401 moment is not fully detailed by these sources; use established Picard-era context and treat unspecified changes as stable unless your story requires otherwise.
- "Post–season 3 era" for play can reasonably mean 2402+ (after the one-year epilogue) unless your game states a different date.
