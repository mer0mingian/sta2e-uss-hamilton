# Crystalline Entity (Memory Alpha Reference)

- **Purpose:** GM-prep reference for a powerful, spaceborne lifeform from *Star Trek* lore.
- **Source:** Memory Alpha, "Crystalline Entity" — https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Crystalline_Entity
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-17
- **Note:** Memory Alpha is a fan-maintained, non-canon wiki. Use this summary as inspiration/reference material; verify against canon sources or campaign needs before treating details as binding.

## At a glance

- Also called a **Crystal Entity**; classified on Memory Alpha as an unnamed species.
- Resembles a large, shimmering snowflake or crystalline structure, reportedly much larger than a starship.
- Travels at warp and can strip all life from a planet or starship by converting organic matter into energy.
- First Federation-encountered entity was responsible for thousands of deaths in the 24th century before being destroyed in 2368.
- By 2381, additional Crystalline Entities were known to Starfleet (per *Star Trek: Lower Decks*).

## Nature and abilities

- **Feeding/conversion:** Functions as a giant electromagnetic collector that consumes living matter and converts it into energy; devastation can leave a world unable to support new life for years (including vegetation and bacteria).
- **Aftereffects:** Attacks are associated with residual bitrious matter and elevated levels of hydrocarbons, lanthanides, and nitrates.
- **Defenses/limits (reported):** Could not penetrate rock lined with both kelbonite and fistrium (both materials required). *Galaxy*-class starship shields were described as impervious to it in encounters depicted on screen.
- **Mobility:** Capable of warp-speed travel.
- **Communication:** Later study attempted communication via graviton pulses; interpretation required time and careful calibration (see *Silicon Avatar* events below).

## Relationships and connections

- **Lore (android):** Allied with the entity in the Omicron Theta attack and later attempted to deliver the *Enterprise*-D crew to it.
- **Dr. Kila Marr:** Federation scientist who studied multiple attack sites (including Forlat III) after losing her son at Omicron Theta; her actions led to the first encountered entity's destruction.
- **Tholians (apocrypha, non-canon):** Memory Alpha's "Apocrypha" section notes that in *Star Trek Online* (non-canon game), another, more powerful Crystalline Entity appears and the Tholians seem to worship and protect it. Treat as non-canon inspiration only, not established screen canon.

## GM hooks (campaign inspiration)

- **Ecological catastrophe:** A feeding event can sterilize a biosphere and leave distinctive chemical traces, creating mystery, humanitarian, and scientific stakes.
- **Countermeasures:** Kelbonite/fistrium-lined refuges and strong shields suggest survivable scenarios, but require preparation and local knowledge.
- **Communication attempt:** Graviton-pulse signaling offers a nonviolent research path with high risk if calibration or intent fails.
- **Legacy and grief:** Survivors, scientists, and affected worlds may carry trauma or pursue dangerous agendas (mirroring Kila Marr's arc).
